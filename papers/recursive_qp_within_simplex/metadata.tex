\title{A Recursive Quadratic Programming Approach Within a Simplex for
Maximum Torque per Current Control}
\author{}
\date{}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
This paper presents a recursive algorithm to solve quadratic minimization
problems constrained to the convex hull of vertices defining a simplex. The
method targets the minimization of copper losses in electric machines by
optimizing stator currents under the Maximum Torque Per Ampere (MTPA)
criterion. The quadratic form minimizes power loss given by
\(x^\top Qx\), where \(Q\) is the diagonal matrix of stator resistances and
\(x\) is the current vector constrained within the feasible region defined by
the convex hull. We discuss the theoretical background and provide the
implementation details of the recursive solver, highlighting its advantages
in guaranteeing feasibility and optimality within the simplex domain.}

\newcommand{\PaperKeywords}{Maximum Torque Per Ampere, quadratic programming,
convex optimization, recursive algorithms, electric machine control.}
