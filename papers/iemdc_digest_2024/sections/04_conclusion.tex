\section{Conclusion}
The node-accurate calculation of cross-sectional areas using prime factor encoding provides an approach that can be implemented with minimal resources, offering low software complexity. The method is highly efficient, as it can be implemented in just a few lines of code. The alternative approach for winding distribution is also advantageous and resource-efficient in terms of software implementation. It directly yields the $\alpha$-axis of the stator-fixed coordinate system. This approach is applicable for any number of phases, making it a versatile solution for various machine configurations. Both methods represent isolated aspects of an analytical development process and will be systematically integrated into the overarching analytical development approach incorporating magnetic equivalent circuits in the full paper.
