\title{Analytical Method for Node-Precise Airgap Reluctance Calculation in
Magnetic Equivalent Circuits\\[0.4em]
\large and an Alternative Winding Distribution Approach for Concentrated
Windings}
\author{}
\date{}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
This paper introduces two methodologies used for the design of electric
drives. The first method analytically calculates node-accurate cross-sectional
areas to determine rotor-position-dependent air-gap reluctances within the
scope of magnetic equivalent circuits. The second method focuses on
determining winding distribution for concentrated windings. Both approaches
are computationally efficient, requiring minimal code and straightforward
operations, making them well-suited for rapid implementation with low
resource consumption.}
