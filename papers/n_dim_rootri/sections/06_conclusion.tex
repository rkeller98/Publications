\section{Conclusion}
This method facilitates the extraction of isocontours from point clouds by interpreting the data as an \(N\)-dimensional spatial domain augmented with a scalar potential. Delaunay triangulation is employed to infer local neighborhood structure, and edge-wise linear interpolation enables precise localization of isocontour surfaces. The approach is applicable to general unstructured datasets and requires no prior mesh structure, making it particularly suitable for processing data from simulations and physical measurements.
