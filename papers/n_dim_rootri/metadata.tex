\title{Isocontour Extraction in $N$-Dimensional Space from Arbitrary Point
Clouds Using Delaunay Triangulation}
\author{}
\date{}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
This paper extends the generalized isocontouring approach introduced by
\cite{OellerichBuescherDegel2023_1000162443} by proposing a more structured
and computationally efficient variant. While the original method utilized
arbitrary planes in three-dimensional space, the present approach restricts
the isocontouring process to hyperplanes normal to coordinate axes. Although
this reduces generality, it enables a systematic and scalable extension to
spaces of arbitrary dimensionality.}
