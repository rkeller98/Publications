\section{Theory and notation}
\label{sec:theory}

Define assumptions and notation before using them. A numbered model equation
can be introduced as
\begin{equation}
  y = f(x;\theta),
  \label{eq:model}
\end{equation}
where every symbol should be defined in the surrounding prose.

Before introducing a transformation or decomposition, state the problem that
motivates it, what becomes simpler after the operation, and what physical or
geometric information might be hidden by it. Include a nearby limiting case
where the familiar conclusion fails.
